🎮 How to Play!

🎯 Level Goal: Earn $150 before closing time!
1
Customer walks in → Click them or the lower zone to enter Seat Select. Green tables glow — click one!
2
Take the order → Click a seated customer. A speech bubble shows what they want. Pick the items and tap Done!
3
Cook food → In the Kitchen panel, click 🍳 Cook. A yellow glow + timer bar shows it's cooking. When done, it glows green!
4
Deliver food → Click 🖐 Pick Up then click the customer's table. Watch the emoji fly across the screen!
5
Collect bill → After eating (watch the progress bar), a 💰 Receipt appears. Click it and solve math before the clock runs out!
⚠️
Lose 4 customers and the level fails. Don't let the patience ring run out!
